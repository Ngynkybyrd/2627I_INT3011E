This study represents generalized Sudoku with cell--value Boolean variables
and compares pairwise, sequential-counter, and bitwise At-Most-One encodings
using the same Glucose3 solver. The dataset contains 60 synthetic instances
at grid sizes 4, 9, 16, and 25 and three clue densities, plus 20 public 9×9
instances. CP-SAT/HiGHS are evaluated on a 16-instance subset; Gurobi, CPLEX
MIP, and CP Optimizer on a 10-instance subset. Three repetitions and a
10-second cutoff covering model construction/loading and solving produce
906 runs: 903 independently checked solutions and three HiGHS timeouts on
one instance. All three SAT encodings solve all 80 instances in every
repetition. On five synthetic 25×25 instances with 30\% clues, median total
times for pairwise, sequential, and bitwise are \PairTotal{}, \SeqTotal{}, and
\BitTotal{} seconds; build costs exceed solving costs. However, rankings change
with grid size; CPLEX MIP has a lower median than pairwise on four common
public instances. These results emphasize construction costs and comparisons
on identical instance sets, without establishing a universally superior
solver or encoding. Uniqueness remains unverified and baseline coverage is
limited.
